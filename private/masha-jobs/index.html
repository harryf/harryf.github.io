Your thirteen questions

  1. Will at least two people know the systems I work on? Who covers when I am on holiday?
  2. Is there a named person responsible for my development, with a plan for months 3, 6 and 12?
  3. Does someone review my code or my work every week?
  4. Is my scope bounded at the start, with expansion as a planned step?
  5. Is the release rhythm closer to monthly than weekly?
  6. Are technical decisions made together, so I contribute without being the one who decides?
  7. Can someone see my workload and rebalance it? Is there no solo on-call?
  8. Would I be glad to explain to a friend what the product does?
  9. Does the company have Swiss or European roots and take privacy and security seriously?
  10. Is the title one I grow into, rather than one I must defend?
  11. Can this job go to 60 percent from August 2027, if I start school?
  12. Does the company support Weiterbildung, with what money and what stay clause?
  13. How long has the team lead led this team, and how does feedback from a junior reach them?

For the RAV

Nachweis der Arbeitsbemühungen

Every card you marked as applied, with the date and the outcome, in the order of the RAV form. Print it, or save it as a PDF from the print dialog, and hand it in with the form. Check the contact column before you print; fill it on the card if it is empty.

You

Your documents

Your CV, your letter and the email text for each application are Google Docs in a Drive folder that Harry shares with you. Your master CV lives there too: keep it complete, and every new application starts from it.

  1. Press "Prepare" on a card. Harry's tool writes the three Docs, usually by the next morning (he can also run it at once).
  2. The card turns "Ready to send" with links CV, Letter, Email and Folder. Read them and change anything that does not sound like you.
  3. In the CV and the letter choose File, Download, PDF. Send the email with the two PDFs attached, then press "I have applied" and fill in the date and the contact.

Nothing on this page holds your address or phone; those are in the Docs only.

Install as an app

On the phone: open the browser menu and choose "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) or "Install app" (Android). On the laptop, Chrome shows an install icon in the address bar. Updates arrive on their own the next time you open it.
