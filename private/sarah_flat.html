🗝️ Getting accepted: a plan for Sarah's situation

Being on social support (revenu d'insertion, RI) makes régies nervous, but it is not a dead end. The trick is to change who decides and what they see. Here is the strategy, most powerful first.

⚠️ Scam check, every time. Never pay a deposit, a "réservation", a key fee or a visit fee before you have visited in person and signed a lease with the gérance. No Twint, no crypto, no Western Union, no "I am abroad, my agent will send the keys". A real gérance has an office you can call; check the company exists on zefix.ch. Facebook and cheap "1 bed for CHF 750" ads are where fraud lives, and people in a hurry to find housing are the target.

1. Go for contract takeovers (reprise de bail) first

Under Swiss law (art. 264 CO) a tenant who wants to leave early must find a replacement who is solvent and ready to take over the same contract. The outgoing tenant, not the régie, does the searching, and they are motivated: every month without a replacement they keep paying. Régies usually accept a takeover candidate with a reasonable file because they lose nothing (no empty month, no re-letting cost) and cannot refuse a solvent candidate without a good reason.

  • Tell the outgoing tenant honestly: "I am on RI, but the CSR guarantees my rent and can pay it directly to the régie. I can also give you two other candidates so you have three to submit." Tenants care about being released, not about the source of the money.
  • Where they are posted: the Facebook group "Reprise de Bail Lausanne", Facebook Marketplace (search "reprise de bail"), Flatfox (many private ads, filter on "Reprise"), reprisedebail.ch, tutti.ch and anibis.ch classifieds.
  • In this page, flats with the purple 🔑 takeover badge are exactly this kind. Use the "takeovers only" toggle above.

2. Make the money question disappear before it is asked

  • Attestation from the CSR. Ask Sarah's assistant social at the Centre social régional for a written attestation that her rent is covered by the RI (within the RI rent ceiling for a single person in the Lausanne region) and that the CSR can pay the rent directly to the landlord (versement direct du loyer). This replaces a pay slip. Find her CSR: vd.ch, trouver un CSR.
  • Rent deposit (garantie de loyer). The RI can cover the deposit or a deposit guarantee, so ask the CSR first. Otherwise a deposit-insurance company means no cash up front: SwissCaution, FirstCaution or goCaution (roughly 4.5 to 5% of the deposit per year). Régies like these because they still get a guarantee.
  • Extrait du registre des poursuites (less than 3 months old, no open debts). Order it today, not when a flat appears: online at vd.ch, Office des poursuites or at the office, about CHF 17. Régies always ask. If it is not clean, tell the CSR: an agreed repayment plan plus the CSR attestation is far better than a surprise, because open poursuites are the number one reason for refusal, more than income.
  • A garant. If a family member or friend with a stable income co-signs, most régies treat the file as normal. Their pay slips and poursuites extract go in the file.
  • Private RC insurance (responsabilité civile) quote or policy in the file. Cheap, and it shows organisation.

3. The dossier: one PDF, ready before the visit

Régies decide on the first complete file. Have a single PDF ready on the phone to send within an hour of a visit:

  • Filled application form of the régie (they all have their own, download it before the visit).
  • Copy of ID or permit.
  • CSR attestation (rent covered, direct payment possible) instead of pay slips.
  • Poursuites extract, under 3 months old.
  • Short cover letter: who Sarah is, that she works at Le Fléau Tattoo in Lausanne, that she is quiet and looking for a long-term home, and that the CSR guarantees the rent. One paragraph, warm, no drama.
  • Optional: a reference from the studio owner and from a previous landlord.

4. Who can help in Lausanne and Vaud

  • Her CSR assistant social is the key person: attestation, deposit, direct rent payment, and they know which régies accept RI tenants.
  • Ville de Lausanne, Coordination et assistance locataires (+41 21 315 74 44): helps people referred by social services get and keep subsidised flats (LLM / logements à loyer modéré) through a "contrat de confiance" that gives the landlord guarantees. Details. Subsidised flats are cheaper than the market and the waiting list is worth joining now.
  • DASL, Dispositif d'aide sociale au logement (Rue de l'Ale 31, via Info sociale at Place Chauderon 4): free workshops on how to search and apply, and support for people at risk of losing housing. Details.
  • Fondation Le Relais, Rel'og (took over Fondation Apollo in 2019): transitional housing with social follow-up for people who cannot access the normal market. relais.ch/relog.
  • ASLOCA Vaud (021 617 16 17): legal advice on rental rights for members, useful if a régie refuses for a discriminatory reason or a takeover goes wrong. vaud.asloca.ch.
  • Housing cooperatives (Logement Idéal, SCHL, Cité Derrière, CODHA): they select on need not income, rents are lower, and they are in the quiet parts of town. Register on their waiting lists now.
  • Caritas Vaud and the Centre social protestant (CSP) Vaud: free social and budget advice, and they know the régies that work with RI tenants. Useful as a second door if the CSR is slow.
  • Live searches with Sarah's filters: Flatfox, Homegate, Immoscout24, Facebook Marketplace, Reprise de Bail Lausanne group. This page is a snapshot; listings in Lausanne go in days, so check these every morning.

5. Quiet and no school: how to check before falling in love with a flat

  • Use the 🏫 badges here: red means a school or kindergarten within 150 m, amber within 300 m. The map layer shows every school so Sarah can see the playground side of a building.
  • Visit at 10:00 on a weekday and at 15:30: that is recess and school-out time. Also stand outside at 20:00 for night noise (bars, trams, main roads).
  • Ask the outgoing tenant directly "what do you hear from the windows?". Tenants leaving are honest because they have no reason not to be.
  • Top-floor flats on courtyard sides, and buildings on cul-de-sac "chemins", are the quietest in Lausanne. Streets with buses (avenues) are louder but have the best commute.
The order that works: 1) get the CSR attestation and poursuites extract this week, 2) join the takeover groups and reply to every takeover ad the same day with the full PDF, 3) register with the Ville de Lausanne subsidised-housing office and two cooperatives as the long-term plan.
