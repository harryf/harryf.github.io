The Remainder

You're not at the start of this graph, and you're not at the end. You're where a comedian would put the punchline: two-thirds in, everything set up, all of it paid for. The remainder is the punchline — and punchlines are short.

Touch the grid to inspect any week. Assumes you turned 52 recently; the squares don't care about the odd month.

Act VII — The Silence

You said it yourself: silence is amazing.

So here is the last interaction. It costs one minute — one of the roughly 11.2 million waking minutes you have left. No text. No numbers. Nothing to optimize, nothing to archive, nothing to build.

Just sit in it. The way you'd want a room to sit in the silence after your bravest joke.

That was one minute. Nothing happened. Nobody laughed. Nothing shipped. Nothing got archived.

And it was still — you said it yourself — amazing.

Awesome is relative. Relative to how awake you were for it. You have 11.2 million more of those. They are not stored anywhere. There is no Jokebox for them.
