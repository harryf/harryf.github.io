The real decision isn't where — it's which comfort you'll trade.

They're 2–10 minute walks from each other, so "the area" barely separates them. What separates them is the tradeoff Sarah kept hitting: two-floor charm vs. air-con vs. a private balcony — no single flat has all three in this district.

Why this city, this district

A City Made of Art

Kraków is Poland's art capital — home of Wyspiański's stained glass and the Młoda Polska movement whose peacock-and-floral spirit colours this page. Kazimierz is its bohemian heart.

Kazimierz nights

Plac Nowy's ringed food hall, candlelit courtyards, klezmer bars and mural-covered lanes — all on the doorstep of every flat here.

10 minutes north

Wawel Castle, the Main Market Square (Europe's largest medieval square), the Cloth Hall and St. Mary's — a flat walk or short tram.

Wyspiański & glass

The Franciscan Basilica's stained glass and the National Museum hold the Młoda Polska masterpieces that make Kraków a pilgrimage for artists.
