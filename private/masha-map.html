  • Bounded tasks inside a curriculum

Application Manager

  • Sole owner of three company systems
  • Single point of contact for vendors, internal IT and stakeholders in several markets; no engineering peers
  • Expected to be the expert from day one
  • Feedback when something broke
  • No stated definition of success

The technical difficulty barely changed. What changed was interrupt load and sole accountability. A first-year professional alone in charge of three enterprise systems is a design with a bus factor of one. Nobody can see the overload except the person carrying it. Leaving was the only signal you had. That reads as judgement, not failure.

For the record: in that seat you still kept three systems running, trained their users, coordinated vendors, and were called "eine Stütze ihrer Abteilung". That is not nothing. It is a lot, done alone.

Two more things your papers show. You paused the apprenticeship once, came back, and finished with a 5.6 and a top-band reference: you finish things. And Rhomberg Sersa is a rail group, so you already carry domain knowledge that rail and mobility employers pay for.

Your filter: ten questions for any job
A tool, built from your own wishes and the two seats. Use it on any advert or interview.
+
  1. Will at least two people know the systems I work on? Who covers when I am on holiday?
  2. Is there a named person responsible for my development, with a plan for months 3, 6 and 12?
  3. Does someone review my code or my work every week?
  4. Is my scope bounded at the start, with expansion as a planned step?
  5. Is the release rhythm closer to monthly than weekly?
  6. Are technical decisions made together, so I contribute without being the one who decides?
  7. Can someone see my workload and rebalance it? Is there no solo on-call?
  8. Would I be glad to explain to a friend what the product does?
  9. Does the company have Swiss or European roots and take privacy and security seriously?
  10. Is the title one I grow into, rather than one I must defend?

Seven or more yes: worth a second look. A no on 1, 2 or 7: the old seat with a new title.

Practical

The rules, the numbers and the questions. Checked in September 2026; check again before you sign anything.

Part-time is normal here
Asking for 80 percent at entry level is an ordinary request
+

Just under four in ten people working in Switzerland work part-time, per the Federal Statistical Office's 2024 labour force survey. In ICT the share is lower, about one in five (IWSB for ICT-Berufsbildung Schweiz, 2020), yet many adverts say "80 to 100 percent". An 80 percent seat is what makes the BM2 and FH part-time models work.

BM2 and FH: the rules
Exam-free entry, schools, cost, part-time models, with sources
+
  • Exam-free BM2 entry with an EFZ average of 5.0 or more, in the school year straight after the EFZ or the one after (zh.ch, Zentrale Aufnahmeprüfung). For a June 2025 EFZ that is August 2026 or August 2027.
  • Technical direction (TALS) in Kanton Zürich: full-time one year at BMS Zürich and BMS Winterthur; part-time two years at BFS Uster and BMS Winterthur (zh.ch, bms-zuerich.ch, bms-w.ch, bfsu.ch).
  • Tuition free for Zürich residents; materials and project weeks roughly CHF 800 to 1,600 (bms-w.ch, bm2.ch).
  • BM plus a related EFZ admits directly to a BSc Informatik at ZHAW, OST, FHNW or HSLU, with no extra practice year. Part-time takes eight semesters at about 50 to 60 percent work (zhaw.ch, ost.ch, fhnw.ch, hslu.ch). OST registration closes 30 April for a September start.
  • Eidg. Fachausweis (ICT-Application Development Specialist): needs two years of development practice after the EFZ, so 2028 at the earliest; the Confederation refunds half the course fees up to CHF 9,500 (ict-berufsbildung.ch, sbfi.admin.ch).
After a resignation
You keep the right to benefit, with penalty days
+

You keep the right to unemployment benefit after resigning, but the fund imposes penalty days (Einstelltage) of between 1 and 60. A resignation without a new job lined up is usually treated as the heavier case, 31 to 60 days. Registration at the RAV must happen by the first day of unemployment. Source: zh.ch, Arbeitslosenentschädigung. If you have not registered, a phone call will tell you what still applies.
