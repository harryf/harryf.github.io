    Read this before driving to Däniken

    1. The ad contradicts itself. The "Unfallfahrzeug" field says no, the description says right-side damage. The last gallery image shows scrapes along the right front door and a dent with paint damage above the right rear wheel arch. The rear quarter panel is a welded panel, not a bolt-on. That is the one thing that decides whether this is a bargain or a trap.
    2. The price is the damage. The cheapest undamaged C 220 d 4Matic estate on AutoScout24 today has 247,000 km and asks CHF 7,999. At 146,800 km, CHF 8,999 only makes sense because of the side damage.
    3. 170 PS means the older OM651 2.1-litre engine, not the 194 PS OM654 that the facelift got in 2018. Not a problem, but it changes what to check (timing chain, injector seals, AdBlue system). Confirm the engine on the Fahrzeugausweis.
    4. Däniken is in Kanton Solothurn, 55 km from Zürich. The car has no plates, so the day needs a plan for getting the Ausweis to a Zürich counter and the plates back to the car. The checklist above spells it out when this car is selected.

    3. What to check on the car

    Order matters. Any declared damage first, then the engine, then the drivetrain, then the paperwork. The engine and gearbox notes below follow the selected car.

    Decides the deal on the Däniken car

    Right-side damage

    • Rear quarter panel above the wheel arch: is it a dent in the original panel, or has it been filled and painted? Paint depth gauge on the quarter, the door, and the sill, compared with the left side. Anything over about 200 microns or wildly uneven means filler.
    • Inner wheel arch and sill from underneath: creases, fresh underseal, or a bent jacking point mean the hit went deeper than the skin.
    • Rear door and tailgate gaps: even gap top to bottom, door closes without lifting, tailgate seals evenly.
    • Right front door: scrapes only, or a dent that pulls the door skin? Open it fully and feel along the crease line.
    • Wheels and alignment: check the right rear alloy for kerb damage. On the test drive the car must track straight with hands off at 80 km/h.
    • Airbag light: must come on with ignition and go off. If any airbag deployed and the ad says "Unfallfahrzeug: Nein", walk away.
    Get a body shop quote for the quarter panel before agreeing a price. A proper repair and paint on the rear quarter plus door is realistically CHF 2,000 to 4,000, which is your negotiating number. On the undamaged alternatives, run the paint gauge over every panel anyway.
    Engines on this page

    What to listen for, by engine

    • OM651 four-cylinder diesel (C 220 / 250 CDI and d, E 220 / 250 CDI and BlueTEC, GLA, CLA, GLK 220): cold-start chain rattle for two seconds means a stretched timing chain; black tar around the injector bases means leaking injector seals; AdBlue warnings on 2015+ cars mean NOx sensor or heater money; DPF must regenerate, so hard acceleration without smoke or limp mode.
    • OM642 V6 diesel (E 350 CDI and BlueTEC, C 350 CDI): oil cooler seals leak into the V (smell of burnt oil, oil on the underside of the intake), swirl flap motor faults, EGR and inlet carbon on high-mileage cars, and the same timing chain check. Strong, smooth, and thirsty at 8 l/100 km.
    • OM626 1.6 diesel (C 180 BlueTEC): Renault-sourced engine, 116 PS. Check the turbo for whistle and the timing chain tensioner. Slow, but cheap to run.
    • M271 / M274 four-cylinder petrol (E 200 CGI, C 180, C 200): the M271 (until about 2013) is known for timing chain stretch and camshaft adjuster rattle, so listen at cold start. Both are direct injection: carbon on the inlet valves, check for rough idle.
    • All of them: two seconds of white smoke on a cold start that clears is fine, persistent is not. Wet lower block or sump means a leak. Turbo whistle or blue smoke under load means a turbo.
    Drivetrain

    Gearboxes and 4Matic

    • 9G-Tronic and 7G-Tronic (torque converter, C, E, GLK): shifts should be invisible on a gentle drive. Flare between 2 and 3, a thump on downshift to 1 when stopping, or hunting at 50 km/h are the warning signs. Ask for proof of the oil and filter service; Mercedes calls for it around 125,000 km, and at the mileages here it is either done or due.
    • 7G-DCT (dual clutch, CLA, GLA): creep from standstill must be smooth, no judder or shunt in stop-start traffic, no hesitation between reverse and drive. Fluid change is due about every 60,000 km. A clutch actuator or mechatronic unit is a four-figure repair.
    • Manual (the Kloten E 220 CDI): clutch bite point high up means a clutch soon; dual-mass flywheel rattle at idle in neutral means more.
    • 4Matic: listen for a whine that changes with speed and not engine revs. On full lock at walking pace there must be no binding or clunking. Front driveshaft CV boots intact.
    • Air suspension (E-Class estates often have rear self-levelling): the car must sit level after a night parked. One Buchs ad openly says "Luftfeder hinten defekt"; that is why it is not on this page.
    • Tyres, brakes, suspension: four matching tyres (mismatch wears the 4Matic transfer case), discs without lips, no knocks over speed bumps.
    Electronics and body

    Assistance systems and rust

    • Camera, blind spot, lane keeping, PDC: switch each one on and confirm it works.
    • Diagnostic scan: if you can bring Xentry or a decent scanner, read all modules. Stored crash events in the airbag module and a mismatched VIN in any module are deal breakers.
    • Keys: two working keys should start the car; one key means budget CHF 300 to 400.
    • Water ingress: lift the boot floor and the spare wheel well, feel the rear footwells.
    • Rust: W212 E-Class and X204 GLK front wings and boot lid seams, W204 C-Class tailgate and door bottoms, sills behind the plastic covers on all of them.
    Paperwork

    Documents that must match

    • Fahrzeugausweis: VIN matches the plate on the B-pillar and the windscreen. Note engine code, Hubraum, Gesamtgewicht, previous Halter.
    • Digital service record (DSB): ask the seller to print it or check it at a Mercedes dealer by VIN. Gaps after 100,000 km are common on cheap cars.
    • Last MFK report: read the Mängel list, not just the stamp. "Ab MFK" in an ad only means the seller says it passed recently.
    • Kaufvertrag: mileage, damage described, "Ab MFK", keys, price. Sign nothing that says "gekauft wie gesehen" without any damage itemised.
    Negotiation

    How to price it

    Under CHF 11,000 and within an hour of Zürich, every diesel Mercedes newer than 2013 has either damage or well over 200,000 km. The exceptions on this page are the 2011 E 200 CGI in Winterthur with 90,000 km (petrol) and the 2015 GLA 200 CDI in Volketswil with 158,000 km. If the Däniken quarter panel is skin-deep and the gearbox has had its oil service, CHF 8,999 is defensible. If not, walk to one of the fresh-MFK cars and use the mileage as your number.
